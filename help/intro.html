Authenticated Forwarding
This page lists every mailbox that forwards a copy of its mail through authenticated forwarding, and the mailboxes whose normal Virtualmin forwarding keeps a local copy and also sends to an external address.

Normal forwarding passes on everything, including spam with no DKIM signature, which Gmail, Outlook and Yahoo reject (550 5.7.26). Those rejections harm this server's reputation. For mailboxes whose Unix user name contains @, normal forwarding with a local copy also creates a mail loop. The Move to authenticated forwarding button switches a mailbox over without changing who receives legitimate mail.

Set or change the addresses of a mailbox on its Edit page in Virtualmin, under Other access settings.
